LAYOUT SENSITIVITY (finding; docs/bench-p9-layout-probe.txt, scripts/p9_layout_probe.sh)
    type_late and type_late_stable with k = 0..4 extra hot loops (each leaving one 1.5 KB trace) prepended execute the
    same instruction stream but take 1.80 G or 2.55 G cycles (+42%) depending only on where in the code arena the trace
    lands (type_late: 2524 2548 1806 1810 1817; stable: 1793 1801 1898 2558 2565 Mcyc).  A trace loop top that is not
    aligned (or a jcc-erratum crossing) is the likely cause (the type_late trace after the re-record is at 0x...e50, the
    stable twin's at 0x...730; unverified).  The set-(d) rule "<= 1.05 x the stable twin" is therefore decided by placement
    luck for type_late (1.417 here, it can be ~1.0 or ~1.42 for the same code); type_entry's 1.080 is NOT layout: it executes
    two exits per entry (19.99 M exits vs 9.996 M for its twin; hot "other" exits 2 vs 1, the integer->float change of
    `a` in the first iteration), about 15 cycles each, which is the F2a side-trace case.  The trace assembler does not
    align loop heads; aligning them (32/64 B) is a candidate for the full build whatever the verdict.
